Why these values?

Mushroom polyhouse target RH: 85–92 %
Oyster and button mushrooms require very high ambient humidity for pin formation and fruiting body development. Below 85 %, surface drying slows pinning. Above 92 %, stagnant moisture promotes bacterial blotch and mould.


Temperature: 24–30 °C
Fruiting bodies develop optimally in this range. Above 33 °C causes heat stress and yield loss. The warning band (30–33 °C) gives operators time to intervene.


LWT offline trigger
MQTT Last Will & Testament is set at connection time. If a node drops unexpectedly, the broker auto-publishes "offline" to its status topic, triggering the UI alert without polling.
